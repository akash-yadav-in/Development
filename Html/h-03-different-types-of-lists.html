Different Types Of List

Unordered List (Represnted by . dots)

  • Apple
    • color : red
    • season : winters
  • Mango
    • color : yellow
    • season : summers
  • Orange
    • color : orange
    • season : summer

Ordered List (Represnted by 1 , 2 , 3)

  1. Apple
    1. color : red
    2. season : winters
  2. Mango
    1. color : yellow
    2. season : summers
  3. Orange
    1. color : orange
    2. season : summer
